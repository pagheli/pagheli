Tomorrow's networks will operate according to what the data is for. I work on getting them there: turning high-level intents into decisions that lower-level RAN functions can act on, and testing those ideas from math to simulation to real radios.

### What I'm building

**[ib-ORAN](https://github.com/YOUR_IBORAN_REPO)** is an open-source ns-3 simulator for intent-based orchestration in Open RAN, aligned with O-RAN Alliance and 3GPP specifications. I designed it at Orange and took it from its first release through 4G LTE and 5G NR support to a USRP-based SDR testbed.

### What I work on

- Intent translation and orchestration in Open RAN
- Goal-oriented and semantic communication
- Multiple access, scheduling and radio resource management
- Optimization and reinforcement learning for wireless systems
- System-level simulation and SDR prototyping

01 — About

A little about me

I'm Pouya Agheli, a telecom researcher passionate about advancing the foundations of modern and future wireless communication systems. My work sits at the intersection of signal processing, network architecture, and protocol engineering.

I enjoy tackling hard problems — whether that's optimizing spectral efficiency, modeling interference in dense networks, or exploring the implications of 6G paradigms. Research, to me, is about asking the right questions as much as finding the right answers.

When I'm not deep in papers or simulations, I write about research ideas, share what I learn, and occasionally contribute to open-source tools in the telecom space.
